What happens next

Putting your site online · Step 3 of 3

Your files are ready

Two files, two places. Follow them in order and your site will be live.

📁 Your website filesEverything visitors will see · 24 MB
🗂️ Your content backupYour pages, posts and settings · 2 MB

What to do with them

  • 1Upload the website filesYour hosting company gives you a folder for this, usually called public_html. Put everything from the first file in there.
  • 2Import the content backupIn your hosting control panel, look for phpMyAdmin, then choose Import and pick the second file.
  • 3Tell your site its new homeYour host will give you a username and password for your content. We've written them into a file called wp-config.php — open it and replace the placeholder details with theirs.

We already changed every web address inside your content, so your links and images will point to the right place.

Something's in the way

Another program is using the web address slot

Only one program on your computer can hand out local web addresses at a time, and something else got there first. Your sites and files are completely safe.

  • If you have XAMPP, WAMP or Laragon installed, close it and try again.
  • Skype and some video call apps do this too. Closing them usually fixes it.
  • Still stuck? Restarting your computer clears it almost every time.

Light Host never changes or removes other programs on your computer.
